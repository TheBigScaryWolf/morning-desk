Data: DefiLlama's public raises database merged with CoinCarp's fundraising list, with duplicates removed (same project within ~2 weeks, or ~6 weeks when the amount matches). CoinCarp shows up to 3 investors per round and doesn't mark leads. New rounds are added by the Monday crawl. Dates are announcement dates: startups often announce a round months (sometimes a year) after the money was committed, so recent activity here lags what investors are actually doing now. A fund that has stopped writing cheques can keep appearing for months. Click a project to search for its announcement, or an investor to filter by them. Valuation estimates assume ~10% sold at pre-seed, 12% at seed, 18% at Series A, 15% at B and 12% at C. They are rough guides, not reported figures.
